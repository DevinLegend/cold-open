Cold Open. Local platform fighter on one flat slab. Player one moves with A and D, jumps with W or Space, aims down with S, attacks with J, grabs with H, and dodges with L. Player two uses the arrow keys, attacks with K, grabs with O, and dodges with P. Hold attack to charge a smash. Percent and knockback launch fighters off the sides or into the pit, which spends a stock. When the match ends, Enter or Space rematches, Escape returns to fighter picks, and Q quits to the title.
